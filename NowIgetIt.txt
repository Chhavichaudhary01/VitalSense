Target Project: VitalSense (https://github.com/alexansh/VitalSense)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. THE 30-SECOND ELEVATOR PITCH (WHAT PROBLEM ARE WE SOLVING?)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
In rural India, healthcare fails because of three broken links:
  1. Low digital literacy and poor internet prevent villagers from booking care.
  2. ASHA workers (community health champions) are buried in paper records.
  3. City doctors and district health officials have zero real-time visibility 
     into village disease outbreaks until it is too late.

VitalSense is a unified, offline-first telemedicine and disease surveillance 
platform. It connects the Patient, the ASHA Worker, the Doctor, and the 
District Health Admin in ONE synchronized loop with zero-internet fallbacks, 
on-device AI prescription scanning, live Google Maps outbreak surveillance, 
and in-app update capabilities.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. THE 4 ROLES & HOW THEY ARE CONNECTED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Instead of 4 separate fragmented apps, VitalSense is ONE app with 4 scoped 
portals that talk to the same unified local-first database:

   ┌──────────────────┐               ┌──────────────────┐
   │ 1. PATIENT       │               │ 2. ASHA WORKER   │
   │ (Villager)       │ ◄───────────► │ (Field Proxy)    │
   └────────┬─────────┘               └────────┬─────────┘
            │                                  │
            │   (Symptoms, SOS, Rx Scans)      │
            ▼                                  ▼
   ┌──────────────────┐               ┌──────────────────┐
   │ 3. DOCTOR        │               │ 4. DISTRICT ADMIN│
   │ (PHC Specialist) │               │ (Surveillance)   │
   └──────────────────┘               └──────────────────┘

Here is how each role works and connects:

1. PATIENT (Accessible, Icon-First Interface):
   * Purpose: For rural villagers with low literacy or limited connectivity.
   * What they do: View permanent offline Health Card (QR identity), log 
     symptoms in simple categories, view prescriptions, and trigger 1-tap 
     Emergency SOS.
   * Connection: When a patient logs a symptom, it instantly routes to the 
     assigned Doctor's triage queue and alerts their village ASHA worker.

2. ASHA WORKER (Community Caseload Manager & Digital Proxy):
   * Purpose: The bridge for villagers without smartphones.
   * What they do: Manages village patient caseloads, scans paper prescriptions 
     using the phone camera (AI OCR), and switches into "ASHA Proxy Mode".
   * Connection: In Proxy Mode, the ASHA worker acts on behalf of any patient, 
     uploading vitals and reports directly into that patient's health record.

3. DOCTOR (Specialist Triage & Clinical Decision Support):
   * Purpose: For clinicians and specialists at Community Health Centers (CHCs).
   * What they do: Reviews prioritized case queues (Mental Health, Maternal, 
     Fever), inspects past patient history/prescriptions, writes medical advice, 
     issues digital prescriptions tied to real-time dispensary stock, and 
     proposes appointments.
   * Connection: The doctor's advice immediately reflects in the Patient's 
     app and alerts the ASHA worker for follow-up care.

4. DISTRICT HEALTH ADMIN (Command Center & Outbreak Surveillance):
   * Purpose: For District Chief Medical Officers (CMOs) and administrators.
   * What they do: Monitors real-time Interactive Google Maps for disease spikes 
     (e.g., 12 fever cases reported in Sundarpura village), checks district 
     pharmacy stock, and broadcasts urgent health directives.
   * Connection: Admin broadcasts instantly appear on all Patient, ASHA, and 
     Doctor dashboards.

3. HOW THE APP COMPONENTS WORK UNDER THE HOOD
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

A. USER INTERFACE LAYER (Jetpack Compose + Glume Design System)
   * Tech: 100% declarative Jetpack Compose with Material 3.
   * Theme: Premium dark mode (~#0D0D12 near-black background with #181822 dark 
     slate cards and #7C5CFC primary purple accents) matching modern clinical 
     software standards (Glume reference).
   * Minimal Top Chrome: Includes 1-tap live role switching (for demoing), 
     connectivity indicator (Online/Offline), and a global Language Toggle.
   * Bilingual Engine (Localization.kt): Full reactive English <-> Hindi (हिंदी) 
     translation across all buttons, forms, and dialogs.

B. STATE MANAGEMENT & NAVIGATION (AppStateHolder + VitalSenseNavGraph)
   * Architecture: Clean Architecture + MVVM (Model-View-ViewModel).
   * AppStateHolder: Central single-source-of-truth holding active user session, 
     language preference, connectivity mode, and active ASHA proxy state.
   * AnimatedContent Navigation: Seamless animated screen transitions without 
     blank flashes.
   * In-App Update Engine: Automatic background check against GitHub releases 
     with a non-blocking floating update banner (Update Now / Later).

C. OFFLINE-FIRST DATA ENGINE (Room SQLite + WorkManager + Firestore)
   * The Problem: Rural villages often have zero cell coverage.
   * How VitalSense Solves It:
     1. Local Reads & Writes: All data is saved immediately to the local 
        Room SQLite database. The app NEVER freezes or blocks the user waiting 
        for an internet connection.
     2. Outbox Sync: When offline, new symptoms or prescriptions are tagged with 
        `isPendingSync = true`.
     3. Background Synchronization: Android WorkManager detects when network 
        connectivity returns and automatically syncs local pending changes to 
        Cloud Firestore in the background.

D. ON-DEVICE AI PRESCRIPTION SCANNER (Google ML Kit OCR)
   * The Problem: Doctors write paper prescriptions that patients lose.
   * How VitalSense Solves It:
     * Patients or ASHA workers can point the camera at a handwritten/printed 
       prescription.
     * Google ML Kit Text Recognition runs locally on-device (zero cloud latency, 
       100% offline).
     * PrescriptionOcrHelper automatically parses medicine names, dosages 
       (e.g., 500mg), frequencies (e.g., 1-0-1), and durations (e.g., 5 days) 
       into structured digital records.

E. INTERACTIVE GOOGLE MAPS OUTBREAK SURVEILLANCE
   * High-accuracy vector Google Maps engine with 360° free pan/drag, multi-touch 
     pinch-to-zoom, and 3 layer styles (Road Map, Satellite, Night Mode).
   * Dynamic teardrop pin markers with color-coded severity (Red = Outbreak Zone, 
     Amber = Active, Green = Stable) and pulsing heat radius circles.
   * Interactive Info Window Card on marker tap with 1-tap advisory broadcast.

F. ZERO-INTERNET EMERGENCY SOS & FALLBACKS (EmergencySosHelper)
   * When a patient taps "Emergency SOS":
     1. Digital Alert: Dispatched via local database / Firestore to assigned 
        ASHA worker and doctors.
     2. Cellular SMS Fallback: Uses Android system intents (`Intent.ACTION_SENDTO`) 
        to pre-populate emergency GPS coordinates and patient medical history 
        via basic 2G SMS to the emergency contact.
     3. 108 Emergency Dialer: 1-tap dialer intent for the national ambulance service.

5. TOP JUDGE QUESTIONS & READY ANSWERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Q1: "Why build one unified app instead of four separate apps?"
A1: "In rural deployments, maintaining four separate apps causes version 
    mismatches, sync failures, and high maintenance costs. A unified codebase 
    with role-based access control (RBAC) ensures seamless data synchronization, 
    instant role switching, and smaller overall download footprint."

Q2: "What happens if there is literally zero internet in a remote village?"
A2: "VitalSense is offline-first. All patient records and health cards are stored 
    locally in SQLite. Prescriptions are OCR-scanned on-device via ML Kit. When 
    internet is missing, Emergency SOS falls back to standard cellular SMS with 
    GPS coordinates. When the phone eventually reconnects to network, WorkManager 
    syncs everything to the cloud automatically."

Q3: "How is patient privacy and security handled?"
A3: "Data access is role-scoped. Doctors only see clinical telemetry and past 
    records; private internal doctor notes are encrypted and invisible to 
    patients; admins only see aggregated anonymized village density statistics 
    without exposing private personal identification."
