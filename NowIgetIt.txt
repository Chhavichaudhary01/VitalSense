1. THE BIG PICTURE: WHAT IS THIS PROJECT?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
VitalSense (SehatSetu) is a single, unified Android app that connects four
critical roles in rural healthcare under ONE roof:
  1. PATIENT: Rural users (low digital literacy, low internet) tracking health,
     accessing an offline Health Card, and using Emergency SOS.
  2. ASHA WORKER: Community health workers managing village caseloads, sending
     notices, and acting as a "DIGITAL PROXY" (logging symptoms, booking
     appointments, and uploading reports on behalf of patients).
  3. DOCTOR: Clinicians reviewing patient cases, prescribing medicines tied to
     dispensary stock, and confirming appointments.
  4. ADMIN: District health officials monitoring early disease/fever outbreaks
     via village heat maps and broadcasting emergency directives.

Instead of building 4 separate apps that would be nightmare to sync, VitalSense
is ONE codebase with a role-aware router and shared database.

3. WHY WAS IT DONE THIS WAY? (ARCHITECTURAL RATIONALE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. WHY STATELOCAL & DECOUPLED UI?
   - The exact UI designs will evolve and change.
   - By keeping all composables stateless and having them consume pure StateFlow
     from the repository, any designer or developer can completely redesign
     the UI, cards, or layouts later without touching the database, queries,
     or business logic!

2. WHY DUAL IN-MEMORY + ROOM PERSISTENCE?
   - In a 2-day hackathon/presentation, live demos can fail if a database query
     lags or network drops on stage.
   - By combining instant in-memory MutableStateFlow with background Room writes,
     the UI is lightning-fast and 100% crash-proof even in Airplane Mode.

3. WHY TOP-BAR ROLE SWITCHER & ASHA PROXY BANNER?
   - During a pitch or live jury review, nobody has time to log out and log in
     4 different times with 4 different passwords.
   - With TopRoleSwitcherBar, the presenter can switch between Admin, ASHA,
     Doctor, and Patient with 1 tap.
   - When in ASHA view, tapping "Act as Proxy" immediately shows how an ASHA
     worker operates the app on behalf of a rural patient with low literacy.

4. WHY PRE-SEED RICH CLINICAL DATA?
   - Blank apps with "No items found" make for terrible demos.
   - Pre-seeding a live outbreak in Sundarpura, real maternal health records,
     and low dispensary stock gives the team an immediate, compelling story
     to present to judges.

5. WHY MODULAR FEATURE PACKAGING?
   - A 5-person team needs to work simultaneously without merge conflicts.
   - Person 2 works in `feature/patient/`
   - Person 3 works in `feature/asha/`
   - Person 4 works in `feature/doctor/` & `feature/admin/`
   - Person 5 works in `feature/prescriptions/`
   - Because Person 1 already built the shared Repository and UI Components,
     team members can just import them and build their features in isolation.
