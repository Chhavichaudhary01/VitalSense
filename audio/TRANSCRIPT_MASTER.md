### Audio File 03 (`3.ogg` — 28.49s)

> This is VitalSense, working with zero internet. Every patient record, vital sign and prescription is written instantly to the local database on the records. No waiting, no server dependency. When the connection drops completely, nothing is lost. The moment, signal returns. VitalSense automatically sings every cute record to the cloud in the background. With no manual action needed from the health worker.

So the most urgent patients are never missed.

### Audio File 06 (`6.ogg` — 27.93s)

> When a case needs a specialist, the doctor raises a referral in seconds, choosing the department and the emergency lift. But unlike a paper slip that often gets lost, vital sense tracks the entire loop. The specialist receives it in their own queue, can accept it, request more history or respond with findings. And those findings flow straight back into the patient's record. Nothing falls through the tracks.

### Audio File 07 (`7.ogg` — 27.27s)

> Paper prescriptions get damaged, lost or mistreated. Writers and scans of prescriptions directly with the phone camera. And on device AI extracts the text, no internet, no cloud upload. So patient data never leaves the device. It even understands medical shorthand automatically, expanding abbreviations so handwritten slip becomes a clean, structured, digital record instantly.

### Audio File 08 (`8.ogg` — 27.25s)

> In a real emergency, every second counts. And ruler areas often have no data at all. One tap on vital sensor source button sends the patient's cheapest location to emergency contact and dispatches are 108 ambulance requests. If there's no internet, the app automatically falls back to direct cellular SMS, carrying the exact cheapest coordinates. So, help is never blocked by a dead network.
